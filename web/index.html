Signal 01 — Direction

SMT divergence

Two instruments driven by the same factor should print the same structure. When one makes a new extreme and its partner refuses to follow, that extreme was more likely local order flow — stops being taken — than a real move in the shared driver. The partner is the control group.

The sweep requirement

A divergence without a sweep is just drift

The strictest filter in the engine. The prior swing has to be taken out intrabar and given back by the close — the wick trades through, the body does not. That is the actual footprint the technique is trying to catch: stops triggered on one instrument, not confirmed on its partner, price rejecting the level. Without it, two charts slowly separating would register as a signal.

Why 1H and 4H

Below 15m it is microstructure, above daily it isn't medium frequency

Lower timeframes produce far more divergences and most are noise. Daily and above produce clean signals but too few to trade, and the holding period stops being medium frequency. 4H carries more weight than 1H, which is why the scoring model credits it an extra point.

The filter that does the work

Correlation is checked before divergence is believed

The dominant failure mode of SMT is correlation breakdown. When two instruments stop moving together, every swing disagrees and a divergence detector fires constantly on nothing. Two unrelated charts disagreeing is not a signal. So the gate runs first, and it shuts.

Returns, not price levels

Trending series correlate even when they have decoupled

Correlating price levels measures shared trend, not shared movement — two instruments drifting up together score high while their day-to-day behaviour has nothing in common. The engine correlates log returns over a rolling 60-bar window, which is roughly two weeks on 4H and a week and a half on 1H. Long enough to be stable, short enough to catch a regime change.

Two thresholds

0.70 to pass, 0.85 to be trusted

Below 0.70 the signal is suppressed entirely, no matter how clean the divergence looks. Between 0.70 and 0.85 it passes but scores two points for pairing quality instead of three. The gap matters most on gold/silver, where the research says treat the strong band as the real floor.

Live example of the gate earning its place

NQ and ES are decoupling right now

The AI-concentration unwind has broken the historical index relationship. In the week to 20 November 2026 the Nasdaq 100 fell 10.0% against the Dow's 8.0%; on 20 November alone NDX fell 5.61% against the Dow's 1.93%. NQ is currently driven by AI capex sentiment and ES by the broader market — different factors, so their disagreement is structural rather than informational. This is exactly when a naive detector produces its most confident and most worthless signals, and exactly when the gate should be left alone.

Signal 02 — Resistance

Multi-timeframe supply & demand

Weekly, daily, 4H and 1H zones built by the same rules on every timeframe, scored out of ten and marked where a lower-timeframe zone sits inside a higher-timeframe one. Direction tells you which way; this tells you where, and where the idea is wrong.

How a zone qualifies

An impulse candle with a body of at least one ATR, up to three legs walked back, then a base of small-bodied candles. The zone is validated on two measures: its height against ATR, and its departure — the move away has to be at least 1.5 ATR, and at least twice the zone's own height. Gap-driven moves are discarded so futures rolls and weekend gaps don't manufacture zones.

How a zone dies

Four ways. No follow-through within two bars of forming. Age past its timeframe's limit. A close beyond the far edge. Or the test count reaching its cap — a level that has been hit three times is not fresh. Weekly zones also break on a daily close and daily zones on a 4H close, so a higher-timeframe level doesn't survive on a technicality after price has clearly gone through it.

Confluence is the product

A high-scoring divergence inside a higher-timeframe zone

SMT supplies the bias. The zone supplies the location and the invalidation level. Either one alone is context, not a setup. The scoring on both signals runs on the same ten-point scale so they can be read together: a 9/10 bullish divergence firing inside a daily demand zone marked ★ is the thing the whole system exists to find. A 6/10 divergence in open space is a note to watch, nothing more.

What we do not claim

No win rate, profit factor or expectancy figure is published anywhere on this site, and none will be until it has been measured on logged out-of-sample signals with costs included. There is no peer-reviewed study of SMT divergence and no published backtest with transaction costs. Any win rate quoted for this technique elsewhere is an unverified in-house number. The mechanism is sound and the statistical idea behind it is well established in pairs-trading literature, but a plausible mechanism is not evidence of an edge.

What you are paying for is the detection, the gate and the scoring — tooling that finds and grades these conditions and tells you how much to trust each one. Not a prediction, and not a signal to copy blindly.
