QuantTools

Simple quantitative tools for analysing the market. Everything runs in your browser.

Position Size Calculator

How many shares to buy so a stop-loss hit only costs a set % of your account.

Returns & Risk Analyser

Paste a list of prices (oldest first) to get return, volatility, Sharpe ratio and max drawdown.

Black-Scholes Option Pricer

Fair value and Greeks for European call and put options.

Theta is per calendar day. Vega is per 1 percentage point change in volatility.

Kelly Criterion

The bet size that grows your account fastest in the long run, given your edge.

Full Kelly is very aggressive. Many traders use half Kelly or less.

Compound Growth

How an investment grows with regular monthly contributions.
